Privacy

What leaves your Mac.

Copper has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of ours. This page says what the app sends, where everything it keeps is, and what this website knows about you.

What the app sends

  • The pages you ask for, and their icons, from the sites themselves.
  • An update check to Copper's GitHub releases, at launch and every six hours. A newer build is downloaded from there too.
  • Nothing you type in the address field until you press Return.
  • No analytics and no crash reports. Copper sends neither, anywhere.

Everything else is off until you turn it on, and then goes only where you pointed it:

  • Agents. Copper's MCP server listens on 127.0.0.1 only, so only programs on this Mac can reach it, and they need its token. What an agent reads from a page goes to the agent you run, on your own subscription. A password, code or card number Copper filled shows to an agent only as •••• (secret, hidden).
  • Jev, only while it runs: the page's controls and your goal go to TypeSafe on your own key, and the model provider you chose writes the text it types.
  • Copper Cloud, only if you link a server you or your team run: the parts you switched on (spaces, settings, bookmarks, history, open tabs), encrypted at rest with your account's key. It never carries passwords.
  • Bitwarden or 1Password, only if you connect one: Copper uses the manager's own command-line client, which talks to that manager's servers as it always does.

Where everything is

On your Mac, unless you linked something above.

WhatWhere it isWho can read it
PasswordsThe macOS login keychain, as ordinary keychain items tagged Copper, or your Bitwarden or 1Password vault if you connect oneCopper. Any other app triggers the system's permission dialog.
History, bookmarks, open tabs, hidden elementsSmall JSON files in ~/Library/Application Support/Copper/You.
Cookies and site dataWebKit's own store for the appThe sites that set them, as in any browser.
ExtensionsUnpacked in Copper's own folder, their data in WebKit's extension storeEach extension, within the permissions you accepted.
Copper Cloud, only if you link itThe parts you switched on, on the instance you linked, encrypted at rest with your account's keyYou, and that instance's operator.
Anything elseNot stored or sent.No one.

A private tab (⇧⌘N) has its own cookie jar and leaves nothing behind when it closes.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no fonts or scripts from anyone else. The one thing it stores is your light or dark choice, in your browser's localStorage, if you press the switch.

The site is served by Amazon CloudFront (copper.exowatt.com) and GitHub Pages (copper-browser.github.io). Like any web host, they see your IP address and the pages you request and keep their own logs; we don't read them for anything about you. Downloads come from GitHub Releases.

Questions

Copper is open source: every line that sends anything is in the repository. Ask anything, or report a problem, in GitHub Issues. When this page changes, the change is in the repository's history.
