    What you just clicked

    The real thing is a native Mac app on WebKit, the engine Safari uses. Same keys, same layout.

    • Spaces

      Each space has its own tabs, colour and icon, and optionally its own sign-ins. Pins are shared by every space, like Arc's Favorites.

    • Split view

      ⇧⌘D puts two tabs side by side on one card. Click a side to work in it.

    • ⌘K finds anything

      Open tabs in every space, by name or site. Type an address and Return goes there.

    • Hide anything, for good

      ⇧⌘H, then click a cookie banner or a newsletter box. It stays gone on that site next time.
